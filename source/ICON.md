# Иконка

Создана встроенным ImageGen. Исходный PNG с прозрачностью: app-icon.png. Windows ICO: app.ico, размеры 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128 и 256 пикселей. ICO встроен в EXE, окно использует значок EXE.

Финальный промпт:

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Windows desktop application icon for 'Мост буфера', a local Wi-Fi clipboard bridge between a computer and an iPhone. Create one polished square 1024x1024 application icon: a deep navy rounded-square tile, a bold mint clipboard silhouette with two opposite transfer arrows integrated into its center and a very small clean Wi-Fi signal above. Modern softly dimensional finish, precise geometric shapes, high contrast, uncluttered, recognizable at 32 pixels. Match a navy and mint app interface. Straight-on view, centered symbol, generous internal padding. No text, no letters, no Apple or Windows brand marks, no surrounding mockup, no shadow outside the tile. Genuinely transparent background outside the rounded-square tile.
